The problem RAG solves

What happens when an LLM needs information it was never trained on?

Without RAG
User

“How much does a consultation at DentCare cost?”

LLM (Knowledge Cutoff)

“I don't know.” (or presents an invented hallucinated price)

VS
With RAG
Evidence A Consultation price · External evidence provided

“The initial dental consultation costs R$ 250.”

User

“How much does a consultation at DentCare cost?”

LLM (Augmented with Evidence)

“The initial dental consultation costs R$ 250.”

i

Core Takeaway: RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) gives the model relevant external information before generation.

03

Documents: The External Knowledge Source

RAG starts with unstructured external information. In this interactive guide, we use a Dental Clinic sample document.

DOC Sample Document: DentCare Clinic Information Plain Text Source

DentCare Clinic is open from Monday to Friday, from 8 AM to 6 PM.

Evidence AThe initial dental consultation costs R$ 250.

Dr. Ana is an orthodontics specialist and works on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Dr. Carlos performs root canal treatments and endodontics procedures.

Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours in advance.

The clinic accepts payments via PIX, credit card, and debit card.

  • RAG starts with external information: PDFs, databases, web pages, or raw text.
  • For this educational walkthrough, we use a simple clean text sample.
In Python Conceptual equivalent python 
document = """
DentCare Clinic is open from Monday to Friday, from 8 AM to 6 PM.
The initial dental consultation costs R$ 250.
Dr. Ana is an orthodontics specialist and works on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
Dr. Carlos performs root canal treatments and endodontics procedures.
Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours in advance.
The clinic accepts payments via PIX, credit card, and debit card.
"""
